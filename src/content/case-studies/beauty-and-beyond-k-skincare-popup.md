## The brief

Bring a group of Korean and luxury skincare brands into one New York room, and make the
weekend produce more than foot traffic — content, coverage and a first US audience the
brands could keep talking to.

## What we did

The Beauty & Beyond Skincare Pop-Up brought K-beauty and luxury skincare brands together for
an immersive experience in Midtown Manhattan. We built the event around a **creator-only
Day 1**, giving content creators prioritised access for product previews and unhurried
filming before the public arrived. The following two days opened to the general public and
to influencers.

Across the floor we ran interactive activations and curated brand experiences rather than a
row of tables — the format that gets people to stay, try, and post.

## Results

- **2,000+ attendees** across the three days.
- A creator-only opening day that produced a content library the participating brands used
  well past the weekend.
- A single venue that let 10+ brands share the cost of a New York launch moment.

Beauty & Beyond is the format we recommend most often to beauty and consumer brands entering
the US: one room, one weekend, a creator day at the front, and the coverage planned before
the doors open.
