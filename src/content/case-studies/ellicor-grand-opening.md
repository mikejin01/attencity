## The brief

Ellicor was opening in four US markets at once. A store opening that reads as a store
opening gets local foot traffic; we wanted each one to read as a designer-toy event that
people travelled for.

## What we did

Ellicor joined hands with Westwood’s **99 Ranch** to host a two-day pop-up celebrating
designer toy culture. We blended food, play, social interaction and community so each venue
became a gathering spot for toy lovers rather than a queue at a till.

Every location featured a **toy exhibition zone**, an **interactive experience area**,
**DIY craft workshops**, and **themed photo walls** — inviting attendees to explore
limited-edition blind boxes and collectible figures in an immersive setting.

## Results

- **7,500+ attendees** across the four locations.
- A repeatable opening kit — zones, workshops, photo walls — that scaled from Los Angeles to
  Sugar Land, Texas without a bespoke build each time.
- Collectible culture as the hook, which put the openings in front of an audience that
  posts about what it buys.
