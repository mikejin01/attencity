## The brief

Two holidays fell in the same week. Rather than pick one, we built a single destination
event in SoHo that gave both audiences a reason to come — and gave 30 small brands a
Manhattan storefront weekend none of them could have afforded alone.

## What we did

We secured the space at 69 Mercer Street and recruited the ecosystem around it:
**over 30 vendors** and **15+ sponsors**, plus a roster of influencers and creators invited
to take part in whole-day workshops — Asian dessert making, candle making and phone-case
design — so the content came out of participation rather than a step-and-repeat.

The press programme was planned before the build. A full release went out through our
distribution network with a tracked pickup report.

## Results

- **8,000+ attendees** across the two days.
- **571 recorded full-page placements**, reaching an **estimated 177M potential audience**,
  with pickups including Business Insider, Benzinga, the Associated Press, StreetInsider,
  AsiaOne, Barchart, The Globe and Mail and Digital Journal.
- A repeatable model: one venue, many vendors, workshops as the content engine, and a
  distribution plan written at the same time as the floor plan.
