## The programmes

Four cross-border communications programmes aimed at US financial and technology desks:

- **iTalkBB × UnionPay** — a payments partnership announcement placed across Business
  Insider, the Associated Press and the Boston Herald.
- **MOYI × China Construction Bank New York** — a banking partnership released through
  Cision / PR Newswire in April 2021.
- **Cypherium × FedNow** — coverage in MarketWatch in March 2022.
- **HSIA** — coverage in Seeking Alpha in August 2022.

## What the work involved

Angle development for a US financial readership, release production written for the desk
rather than for the client, and syndication through wire and business-media endpoints with
tracked pickup reporting.

## Results

Named placements across **Business Insider, the Associated Press, Yahoo Finance,
MarketWatch, Seeking Alpha, Cision / PR Newswire and the Boston Herald**.
