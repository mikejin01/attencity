## The brief

MiPalette’s product is a travel-friendly magnetic palette — you build the compact you
actually carry. That is a story you cannot tell in a listing photo. It needs hands.

## What we did

We hosted the pop-up at MEC (Gorgeous Beauty) on 34th Street and designed the day around
**hands-on DIY customization stations**. Guests worked through a full spectrum of magnetic
colour pans, mixing and matching eyeshadows, blushes and bronzers into one streamlined
palette designed for life on the go.

Around the build stations we ran product trials and interactive content moments so beauty
lovers and creators had a reason to film, not just to browse.

## Results

- **300+ attendees** in a single eight-hour day.
- A room of finished, personalised compacts — the strongest possible demonstration of a
  modular product.
- Creator content built around the customization moment rather than the packaging shot.

The lesson generalises: when the product’s advantage is an experience, the launch should be
an experience too.
