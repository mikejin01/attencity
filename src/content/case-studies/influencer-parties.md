## The brief

Brands without an established creator network face the same cold-start problem: you can pay
for one post at a time, or you can build a relationship. Paying per post is slower and more
expensive than it looks.

## What we did

We plan and execute multiple influencer-themed events a year, **each bringing together 70+
influencers**, and run them as content production rather than hospitality. Partner branding —
in this series, Attencity × StarHub at The Rose New York — is built into the environment so
every frame carries it without anyone reading a script.

We form deep partnerships with creators who have millions of followers, and pair them with
the mid-tier voices a category actually trusts. For clients lacking a stable influencer
network and needing to boost online visibility, we build an efficient, scalable influence
pipeline through systematic sourcing and content planning.

## Results

- **70+ influencers per event**, in person, in one night.
- A month of co-created photo and video content from a single evening.
- A standing creator roster that partner brands can draw on for the next campaign instead of
  starting outreach from zero.
