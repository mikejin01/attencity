## The brief

Build a nightlife property, not a one-off party — something with a name people recognise,
that can be sold to sponsors again next quarter.

## What we did

KEDM Party and KEDM After Dark are immersive nightlife event series built around Asian
culture and K-pop culture, held multiple times in New York. Each event blends **dance crew
performances** and spontaneous dance interaction with party atmosphere for deep engagement
and energy — amplified further with creative giveaways.

## Results

- Each event draws roughly **300–500+ attendees**.
- One night drew **2,800+ attendees in six hours** and generated 300+ page visits, with
  strong engagement both online and offline.
- Together, these events validate an effective promotion strategy, strong community
  interest, and a repeatable model for nightlife culture activations that brands can attach
  to.
