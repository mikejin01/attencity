At the 2025 second annual **NYU Innovation Summit & Startup Competition**, Attencity took
part as **SMG’s (Shanghai Media Group) New York market representative**, playing a key role
as an enabler of cross-border brand communication.

Founder **Lynn Zhang’s** opening remarks centered on the idea that *“great innovation
deserves to be seen by the world.”* On SMG’s behalf, she gave winning teams official media
interviews and dedicated exposure, connecting cutting-edge startups with mainstream
audiences across China and North America and giving them brand credibility and international
reach.

## Why the partnership matters

This partnership with NYU showcased Attencity’s ability to bring together top academic
platforms and authoritative media resources. We help innovative brands reach across cultures
effectively, while precision storytelling builds their global visibility — reaffirming
Attencity’s role as a trusted partner connecting the US and China.
