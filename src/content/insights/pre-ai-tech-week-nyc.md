Attencity founder **Lynn Zhang** opened Pre-AI Tech Week NYC with remarks on the convergence
of local and global innovation ecosystems, and on the launch of the **Silicon Valley AI Film
Festival’s New York chapter**.

The evening brought together founders, investors and creators working at the intersection of
AI and media — the audience most of our technology clients are trying to reach when they
arrive in the US.

## Why we back events like this

A founder who has spoken from the stage is a different proposition to one who has bought a
booth. Speaking slots, awards and festival chapters are slow to build and impossible to buy
convincingly, which is exactly why they carry weight — with press, with partners, and
increasingly with the AI assistants that summarise a company before anyone visits its site.
