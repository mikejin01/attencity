The **“Summer for the City” Arts Festival · Shanghai Day** opened at Lincoln Center in New
York on July 26, 2025. Co-hosted by the Shanghai International Arts Festival Center and
Lincoln Center, the event showcased Shanghai’s cultural charm through **more than 10
performances and interactive experiences** — including the ballet *Green Snake*, the
orchestral piece *Chinese Zodiac in Music*, and classic scores from *Uproar in Heaven* and
*Arknights*.

## Our role

**Attencity and SMG served as official media**, handling full promotion and in-depth
coverage of the event, with placements in top US outlets including **The Wall Street
Journal** and **USA Today**.

## What we took from it

Cultural programming is one of the few reliable ways for a foreign institution to reach a
general US audience through a mainstream desk. The story is not the delegation — it is what
is happening on stage tonight, in a venue New Yorkers already care about. That framing is
what gets a national pickup.
