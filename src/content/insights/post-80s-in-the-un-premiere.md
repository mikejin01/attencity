**“Post-80s in the UN,”** a bilingual (Chinese–English) documentary produced jointly by UN
Global Communications and Shanghai Media Group’s International Communication Center, held its
world premiere at **UN Headquarters in New York on September 2, 2025**.

As a strategic co-organizing partner, **Attencity joined SMG** in celebrating the
documentary’s successful premiere.

## Why we were there

As a marketing agency built for US localization, we are rooted locally while building brand
influence, cohesion and reach on a global scale. We believe in the power of cross-cultural
exchange, and remain committed to telling China’s story on the global stage — helping
Chinese companies expand abroad.
