On September 23, 2025, the **Annual First Ladies Luncheon** was held during UN General
Assembly week in New York, bringing together diplomacy and fashion in support of women’s and
children’s welfare worldwide.

Attencity was on hand as former UN Secretary-General **Ban Ki-moon**, Nobel laureate
**Muhammad Yunus**, philanthropist **Amanda Schumacher** and other honorees were recognized
at the event.

## Our role

**Attencity and SMG attended as official media**, covering the event in full and conducting
in-depth interviews with guests. It marked another milestone for Attencity as a US-based
marketing agency — with media reach and influence spanning fashion, diplomacy and education.
