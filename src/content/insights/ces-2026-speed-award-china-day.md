The 2026 CES · Speed Award took place January 6–9, 2026 in Las Vegas, Nevada.

Since 2024, when **SMG × Attencity** became CES’s sole official Chinese-language media
partner, we have led the creation, organization and on-the-ground execution of the
**“Speed Award World Tech Summit · China Day.”** As Shanghai Media Group’s official
strategic partner in North America, Attencity is the only US-based partner co-organizing the
event with SMG.

## This year's programme

This year’s Speed Award featured **3 tech forums and panels** and presented **5 award
categories** — Top Innovation Award, Outstanding Engineering Award, Excellence in Design
Award, Global Branding Award, and Smart Living Award — honouring **15 companies**, including
TCL, Hisense, Segway and LumiMind.

## Why it matters for brands going global

CES is where a hardware brand’s US narrative is set for the year. For Chinese companies, the
gap is rarely the product — it is that the English-language and Chinese-language stories are
produced by different people, on different timelines, and end up saying different things.
Running both from one partner is the whole point of the China Day format.
