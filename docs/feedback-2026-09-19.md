## Notes

- The two app-exported QR cards (Instagram, Xiaohongshu) could not be machine-decoded during the build — they use decorative modules and a centre logo that OpenCV's reader will not parse. They are the clients' own official cards and unchanged by this work, but worth a scan on a phone before launch. The three generated codes were decoded and verified.
- `industry` values are constrained to `INDUSTRIES` in `posts.js` (Beauty, Tech, F&B, Retail, Entertainment, Finance). Add to that list before using a new one, or the filter chip will not appear.
- The "what we did" chips only show service labels that at least one published case study carries, so a chip is never shown with nothing behind it.
